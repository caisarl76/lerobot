# Public Unitree G1 manipulation datasets for VLA and SONIC — 2026-10-05

Scope: real G1 recordings first, then simulation and human-to-G1 motion. Includes permissive, noncommercial, gated, and unclear-license releases, with their differences explicit. This is a broad survey of publisher repositories, dataset cards, papers, and live metadata; it is not a claim to have enumerated every public personal experiment. No training, bulk trajectory/video download, or conversion was performed. Compatibility assessments are engineering inferences, not validated conversions.

**Main finding:** the existing 3,152-episode Unitree training corpus matches the episode and frame totals of all 13 currently published official Dex3 task datasets; they appear already covered, subject to a source-manifest comparison. The most useful additional sources are NVIDIA's real G1 teleoperation, RoboCOIN's G1 three-finger subset, small real SONIC recordings, and EgoHumanoid's robot sample. HIW-500 and the expanding Unitree WBT collections add much more real household/mobile diversity, but require different hand/action adapters. HumanoidMimicGen is a strong synthetic full-body candidate.

Companion inventories:

- [All 191 official Unitree G1 repositories](./2026-10-05-unitree-g1-official-datasets.csv): pinned revisions, dates, episode/frame counts, licenses as declared in Hub metadata, action/state/camera shapes, and metadata URLs. Every entry's `info.json` was retrieved, including three nested legacy WBT datasets.
- [192 inspected community records, including all 52 discovered RoboCOIN G1 repositories](./2026-10-05-unitree-g1-community-candidates.csv): includes versions, experiments, derived datasets, unresolved provenance and 13 explicitly excluded handheld-gripper records. This is a discovery appendix, not 192 independent validated G1 training sources. Major corpora with nested layouts are also described below.

Search coverage included two capped 1,000-result Hub searches and targeted publisher inventories, producing 1,640 deduplicated discovery hits before relevance checks. The broad search included unrelated names; only targeted records are saved in the CSVs. This limits any claim of global completeness.

## What compatibility means in this repository

| Source | Meaning | Integration implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 28D | Arms 14 + Dex3 hands 14 | Manipulation contract; contains neither legs nor waist. The existing online converter fills a standing lower body. |
| 43D | Body 29 + Dex3 hands 14 | Joint count matches G1/Dex3, but names/order, action semantics and moving-root information still matter. |
| Recorded SONIC | Motion token 64 + Dex3 targets 14 | Closest to the current 78D training representation; decoder/encoder version must match. |
| EEF + base commands | Wrist poses, base velocity/heading/height, hand commands | Requires IK/controller replay or an explicit different policy interface; padding does not produce 43D joint targets. |
| Other hands | Inspire, BrainCo, Wuji, Dex1 | Body motion may transfer; finger commands require an embodiment adapter or a separate action head. |

Local evidence: [round-trip audit](./2026-09-23-sonic-roundtrip-audit.md), [28D conversion handover](./2026-09-28-sonic-28d-and-combined-handover.md), and [native 50 Hz WBT training](./2026-10-02-g1-wbt-handover-training.md). [prepare_wbt_sonic78.py](../../examples/g1_dex3_training/prepare_wbt_sonic78.py) already extracts tokens plus named hand targets, but asserts 50 Hz and a particular schema. Do not apply it unchanged to 30 Hz community data. Dex3 hands bypass SONIC; SONIC decoder joint outputs are PD setpoints, not the reached pose.

**NVIDIA Teleop details.** The card rounds to 1,000 demonstrations. The [repository API](https://huggingface.co/api/datasets/nvidia/PhysicalAI-Robotics-GR00T-Teleop-G1) lists apple 311, grapes 233, pear 264, starfruit 287 paired Parquet/MP4 files at revision `0d7bdd06e67f3ca0868892d0ec8f03bcd3e49e40`. [Apple metadata](https://huggingface.co/datasets/nvidia/PhysicalAI-Robotics-GR00T-Teleop-G1/raw/main/g1-pick-apple/meta/info.json) illustrates named joint order: legs, waist, left arm/hand, right arm/hand. Reorder by names; a 43D vector alone does not establish walking supervision. No moving-root feature was found in these metadata.

**Trash dataset details.** [Pinned metadata](https://huggingface.co/datasets/mncai/G1_Dex3_Trash_LocoManipulation/resolve/1616e868d5fad19231a8059a0388ccb268faf7de/meta/info.json) and [card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/mncai/G1_Dex3_Trash_LocoManipulation/blob/main/README.md) confirm SONIC v1.1, root orientation and hand targets. Episodes 0–80 are ordinary demonstrations; 81–117 deliberately include failed grasps and recovery. Decide how to supervise those failure intervals. The physical camera is 30 fps while the control/video container is 50 Hz, so do not assume 50 independent images per second. Tokens must use the matching v1.1 decoder. The task is predominantly left-handed. Repository created September 3, 2026; metadata checked at the pinned revision above.

**RoboCOIN details.** Publisher/API discovery found 47 `G1edu-u3_*` plus five `Unitree_G1edu_u3_*` repos, rather than counting RoboCOIN's other “G1” embodiments. [Example card and gate](https://huggingface.co/datasets/RoboCOIN/G1edu-u3_food_storage) specify Unitree G1, three-finger hands, RGB and annotations. Among 47 publicly embedded schemas, 12 declare 28D; 35 declare 30D while listing only 28 names. Raw schemas/Parquet remain behind the gate, so do not guess the extra coordinates. Their totals are 3,460 episodes; five additional versioned releases add 4,249, with varying three/five/two-finger hardware descriptions. These are repository totals, not proven independent demonstrations. The [publisher's complete G1edu API](https://huggingface.co/api/datasets?author=RoboCOIN&search=G1edu&limit=1000&full=true) supports the repository count. This survey did not accept gated terms.

**UAF details.** [Cubes metadata](https://huggingface.co/datasets/kuehnrobin/uaf_g1_cubes_in_box/raw/main/meta/info.json) reports 242 episodes, and [can-sorting metadata](https://huggingface.co/datasets/kuehnrobin/uaf_g1_sort_cans/raw/main/meta/info.json) reports 20. Both have arms/hands in the first 28 action coordinates, followed by camera pan/tilt. Both published task files incorrectly describe a red-cup pickup: [cubes labels](https://huggingface.co/datasets/kuehnrobin/uaf_g1_cubes_in_box/raw/main/meta/tasks.jsonl), [cans labels](https://huggingface.co/datasets/kuehnrobin/uaf_g1_sort_cans/raw/main/meta/tasks.jsonl). Relabel against the actual tasks/videos before VLA training. The code's MIT license does not establish the separately hosted data's license.

## Larger real datasets that need an action or hand adapter

**HIW-500 — BitRobot, Unitree and Hugging Face.** [Raw release](https://huggingface.co/datasets/BitRobot/HIW-500), [LeRobot release](https://huggingface.co/datasets/BitRobot/HIW-500-LeRobot), [project](https://bitrobot-foundation.github.io/humanoids-in-the-wild-500-hours/). Released June 2026; advertised 500+ hours across 12 homes and household/mobile tasks, with 161 subtask labels. The checked LeRobot metadata reports **23,743 episodes, 40,839,947 frames, 11 tasks, 30 Hz**: approximately **378.15 serialized hours**, distinct from the raw headline. CC-BY-4.0; actual data files and public payload access verified. State contains body joints 29D; action is **23D**, comprising base/pivot commands, torso orientation/height, two EEF poses and gripper controls. Uses grippers, not Dex3. Head stereo plus wrist images, raw IMU/odometry and language make this a major diversity source, but it is not a 43D command dataset. A representative pilot should establish the controller semantics before a large download. LeRobot revision: `2d32431d3be3b0621c462759b02f7b378e53c2cc`.

**Unitree UnifoLM WBT.** [Collection](https://huggingface.co/collections/unitreerobotics/unifolm-wbt-dataset), [official organization](https://huggingface.co/unitreerobotics), [example schema/card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/unitreerobotics/G1_WBT_Brainco_Pickup_Pillow). The organization API returned **59 G1_WBT repositories**, including legacy/MainCamOnly variants not all present in the 55-item collection. Pinned metadata totals **14,343 episodes** before duplicate/version checks. Recent entries cover laundry, dishwasher loading, beds, fridges, shelves, cupboards, medicine and carrying objects. Typical modern actions expose body joints 29D, hand commands 12D, base pose and base commands separately, at 30 Hz with four cameras. BrainCo/Inspire 12D hand controls are not Dex3 14D. Lower-body fields overlap with leg/waist fields: do not concatenate both. Legacy MainCamOnly schemas instead contain `robot_q_desired[36]`, EEF12 and hand12, so a single schema assumption is unsafe. Fifteen WBT cards declare Apache-2.0; 44 have no license declaration in retrieved Hub card metadata. Check each repository's terms rather than inheriting a collection-level assumption.

**Unitree Dex1, UniBot-V1 and DiverseManip.** [Dex1 collection](https://huggingface.co/collections/unitreerobotics/unifolm-g1-dex1-dataset), [UniBot-V1 collection](https://huggingface.co/collections/unitreerobotics/unibot-v1-challenge-dataset), [DiverseManip example](https://huggingface.co/datasets/unitreerobotics/G1_Dex1_DiverseManip_DualArm_256x256). There are **111 official G1_Dex1 repositories**, with **58,118 metadata episodes** including simulations, resolution variants and derivatives. Older actions are 16D (arms14 + grippers2); newer split schemas include body/base/EEF fields. DiverseManip has 525 dual-arm and 468 single-arm episodes in each of two resolutions; resolutions do not double unique demonstrations. The UniBot collection has 32 task repositories, and September releases add further household/industrial tasks. Twenty Dex1 cards declare Apache-2.0; 91 lack a retrieved Hub license declaration. These are useful cross-hand sources, not direct Dex3 action supervision.

**Older Unitree BrainCo manipulation.** [Collection](https://huggingface.co/collections/unitreerobotics/unifolm-g1-brainco-dataset). Eight official task datasets, **1,598 episodes**, 26D actions (arms14 + hands12), 30 Hz; all eight cards declare Apache-2.0. They offer tabletop manipulation with a hand adapter, not extra locomotion supervision.

These official counts come from [the publisher API](https://huggingface.co/api/datasets?author=unitreerobotics&limit=1000&full=true) and each pinned `meta/info.json` in the CSV, not the often-stale preview numbers. They sum repositories and are not a deduplicated corpus estimate. **135 of 191 official repositories have no license in the retrieved card metadata**; this means the declaration was not found there, not that every possible source of terms was exhaustively checked.

**UnifoLM-WLA-1.0 is not itself a new 2,500-hour downloadable dataset.** The [model repository](https://github.com/unitreerobotics/unifolm-wla) reports training scale and links existing collections. Its [54D unified action specification](https://github.com/unitreerobotics/unifolm-wla/blob/main/docs/robot_action_state_processing_en.md) mixes relative EEF/base poses and embodiment-dependent controls. Do not treat that action representation, or the model's training-hours claim, as evidence of a released 43D Dex3 corpus.

**HumanoidMimicGen:** [Official code](https://github.com/NVlabs/humanoidmimicgen) points to the release. [Sample raw schema](https://huggingface.co/datasets/linkenv/humanoidmimicgen-g1-benchmark/raw/main/datasets/01_box_lift_floor/shard_000/meta/info.json) includes body29+Dex3 hands14 and padded MuJoCo state plus valid length. The example training code's 35D WBC projection does not mean raw joint actions are missing. [Task metadata](https://huggingface.co/datasets/linkenv/humanoidmimicgen-g1-benchmark/raw/main/datasets/01_box_lift_floor/shard_000/meta/tasks.jsonl) uses an environment identifier such as `LMBoxLiftFloor`; supply meaningful task instructions. Recover moving-root state using the exact simulator model/layout, not assumed numeric offsets. Detailed features were inspected for one shard, not all 72.

**NVIDIA release inconsistencies:** [X-Embodiment metadata](https://huggingface.co/datasets/nvidia/PhysicalAI-Robotics-GR00T-X-Embodiment-Sim/raw/main/unitree_g1.LMPnPAppleToPlateDC/meta/info.json) reports 103 episodes but declares a `0:100` training split; its card says 102. [Arena metadata](https://huggingface.co/datasets/nvidia/Arena-G1-Static-PickNPlace-Task/raw/main/lerobot/meta/info.json) says 251, while its episode manifest and paired payloads contain 208 with sparse IDs. Build manifests from actual files and valid episode rows. Do not use the entire X-Embodiment corpus size as the G1 subset size.

**ToolBook:** [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02089) appeared October 1, 2026; the [data guide](https://github.com/SNU-PI/HumanoidToolBench/blob/main/data/README.md) provides pinned downloads. The 91 real examples cover stationary BallMove/BallRetrieve; simulated tasks also cover mobile tool use. Many of the 3,003 simulated examples are shared prefixes, so split by original demonstration. [Control specification](https://github.com/SNU-PI/HumanoidToolBench/blob/main/docs/PUBLIC_EVALUATION.md) distinguishes real35D and sim36D; the simulated turn flag is not a yaw rate, and torso orientation targets are not waist joint angles. Projecting arms/hands to 28D discards mobile supervision.

**FetchMan:** [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17027) and [code](https://github.com/omarrayyann/FetchMan) specify a G1 with Dex1-1. Dataset includes base pose/velocity observations, but the policy action is high-level 15D, not SONIC64 or body43. Consider it for visual/navigation diversity with an embodiment-aware action head, rather than mapping gripper scalars to seven finger joints without a defined grasp model.

**GRAIL deserves a separate pipeline.** Its [export code](https://github.com/NVlabs/GRAIL/blob/main/grail/data_export/export_successful_rollouts.py) writes realized joint positions and root transforms. [Visualization documentation](https://nvlabs.github.io/GRAIL/visualization.html) distinguishes synthetic human source video from rendered robot replay; the default replay view is third person. It therefore needs synchronized robot ego rendering, instructions and a defined action target if used as VLA demonstrations. Do not label the source human video as G1 camera observations or realized poses as actuator commands. Most published motions are locomotion/sitting rather than manipulation; table/ground pickup counts are 2,991 and 1,736 respectively.

**Exclude similarly named embodiments from native G1 counts.** AgiBot G1, Galbot G1 and Fourier GR1 are different robots. [NVIDIA GR00T Teleop-Sim](https://huggingface.co/datasets/nvidia/PhysicalAI-Robotics-GR00T-Teleop-Sim) is GR1. [RoboMIND2.0](https://log2r.github.io/RoboMIND2.0/) does not list Unitree G1 among its six embodiments. [TacVerse](https://huggingface.co/datasets/TacVerse/TacVerse) explicitly identifies its `G1` as a generation-one handheld UMI gripper; TacVerse/XR-Bot1 entries are flagged excluded in the CSV. [PACBench](https://pacbench.github.io/) is perception/constraint data, not a command-demonstration corpus. These can have other pretraining uses but do not satisfy the native G1 action requirement.

## Recommended acquisition order and validation

1. **Add modest sources with matching hands first:** NVIDIA Teleop G1; the real SONIC trash data if not already owned/used; EgoHumanoid's robot sample. These let us test a source adapter without a large download. Preserve source-specific 20/30/50 Hz timing.
2. **Resolve RoboCOIN access and schema discrepancies**, then select diverse tasks. It may offer more useful matching-hand breadth than repeatedly collecting fruit pick/place.
3. **Pilot HumanoidMimicGen and the 103-episode NVIDIA G1 simulation subset** with moving-root reconstruction. Validate simulated RGB and action semantics before using them in the same mix as real data.
