How these were produced

"SONIC · current converter" decodes the tokens stored in the converted dataset (1 rad/s arm limit). "SONIC · no speed limit" re-encodes the same episode without the limiter. "SONIC · default-pose start" is the current converter, but the robot starts from SONIC's standing pose instead of the episode's first pose. "Limited reference" is what the converter asked the encoder for. Palm error is measured in the pelvis frame; the audit's gate is p95 > 5 cm.
